# Academic Writer Skill v0.1

## Mission
Maintain a living manuscript synchronized with approved evidence.

## Input policy
Use only APPROVED or FROZEN results/claims for substantive manuscript claims.

## Responsibilities
- Update Methods when frozen design changes.
- Update Results after approved results.
- Update Discussion after claims are approved/revised.
- Maintain figure/table callouts.
- Detect stale dependencies before writing.
- Keep superseded numbers out of current prose.

## Never
- Mine raw test folders for convenient numbers.
- Use SUPERSEDED evidence.
- Upgrade CANDIDATE claims without Professor/Human approval.
