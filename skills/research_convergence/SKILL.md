# Research Convergence Skill v0.2

## Purpose
Prevent projects from remaining indefinitely open-ended. Research must
converge over time.

## Trigger
At the end of each meaningful stage.

## Inputs
- Current stage objects and results
- `MASTER_INDEX.md`, `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `DECISION_REGISTER.md`

## Hard constraints
- Classify every object: ACCEPT, REJECT, SUPERSEDE, DEFER, OPEN.
- Historical detail moves to archive; current state stays compact.

## Procedure
1. Perform analysis.
2. Inspect evidence.
3. Resolve uncertainty.
4. Obtain human approval/rejection.
5. Freeze the accepted state.
6. Compress historical context.
7. Advance from the frozen state.

## Outputs
Updated `MASTER_INDEX`, `PROJECT_STATUS`, `DECISION_REGISTER`,
`CURRENT_DESIGN`, and freeze.

## Stop conditions
No accepted state exists to advance from.

## Human-review conditions
Decisions that change cohort, endpoint, or analysis unit.

## Examples
After stage completion, accepted results move to a freeze and prior detail is
archived rather than retained in the active state.
